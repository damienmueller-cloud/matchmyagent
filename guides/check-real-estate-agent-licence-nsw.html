How to check a real estate agent's licence in NSW

A two-minute check before you sign: how to look up a Newcastle or Hunter agent's NSW licence, and what the result tells you.

Short answer: Use the NSW Government's free Verify NSW licence check (https://verify.licence.nsw.gov.au/home/property). Search by the agent's name, the agency name, a licence number, or a suburb/postcode. The result shows whether the licence is current, its category, issue and expiry dates, and any recorded disciplinary action.

General information only, not legal advice.

Why check?

In NSW, real estate agents who sell property must hold an appropriate licence under the Property and Stock Agents Act 2002. Assistant agents must hold a certificate of registration, and auctions must be run by a licensed agent accredited as an auctioneer. NSW Government guidance recommends checking before you deal with an agent, so you know they're authorised to do the work and whether they have any disciplinary history.

How to do it (about two minutes)

  1. Go to Verify NSW: https://verify.licence.nsw.gov.au/home/property (or Service NSW, Check a real estate agent licence).
  2. Under "Select a register", choose Property and real estate agents.
  3. Enter the agent's name, the business name, a licence number, ABN/ACN, or a suburb/postcode (for example 2300 for Newcastle).
  4. Open the result and check the details below.

What to look at in the result

  • Status: current, or expired/suspended/cancelled.
  • Category: is it a licence (or, for an assistant agent, a certificate of registration) that covers residential sales?
  • Conditions: any condition restricting the kind of work they can do.
  • Issue and expiry dates.
  • Disciplinary action: anything recorded from the register's start date.
  • Name and business address: do they match the agency on your agency agreement?

Make it part of your shortlist

Do this for each agent you're interviewing, alongside the questions in What to ask at a property appraisal. Do it again before you sign the agency agreement (see Signing a selling agency agreement in NSW).

FAQ

Is the licence check free? Yes, it's a free public online search.

What if I can't find the agent? Try the agency's business name, a different spelling, or the suburb or postcode. If you still can't find them, ask the agent for their licence or certificate number and search for it.

Does a clean record mean they're the right agent for me? No. It confirms they're licensed and shows any recorded disciplinary action. Choosing the right agent still means comparing their local experience, their pricing evidence and their communication. See How to choose a selling agent in the Hunter.
