Seller FAQ

Common questions from people selling a house. Free for sellers, anywhere in Australia.

Is Match My Agent free for sellers?

Yes. Match My Agent is free for sellers. There are no payments on this site.

How does Match My Agent work?

Tell us your suburb, anywhere in Australia. We connect you with one local real estate agent who can help you sell. You talk to them and you decide — you’re in control the whole way.

Which areas do you cover?

Match My Agent works Australia-wide. Example area pages include Newcastle, Lake Macquarie, Maitland, Cessnock and Port Stephens in the Hunter, NSW — but you can use any suburb.

How many agents will contact me?

We connect you with one selected matched agent. Your enquiry may be shared with that one agent for the purpose of introducing you.

How do I pick a selling agent?

Start with fit, not slogans. Ask who will actually run your campaign day-to-day, how they recommend a price range for your suburb, what recent comparable sales inform that view, who handles opens and buyer follow-up, how you will communicate, and what costs to expect before listing. Watch for red flags: guaranteed prices with no context, pressure to sign immediately, vague answers about who works your file, or “#1” claims without verifiable criteria.

How does agent commission work?

Commission is set in the agency agreement between you and the agent — confirm the percentage or fee structure in writing. Also ask what marketing costs are upfront and what is included versus optional. Other selling costs to budget for include preparation, legal or conveyancing fees, and settlement adjustments such as rates, water and strata.

Am I under any obligation if I request an appraisal?

You should not be. After the appraisal it’s still your decision — compare advice, not just headline dollars.

Do agents pay to be matched?

Agents can choose paid MatchMyAgent membership plans for software and workflow tools, and a Verified Profile is free for every eligible agent. Paying does not guarantee an agent a lead, and sellers stay free.

Is Match My Agent a listing site?

No. It’s not a property listing site, not a place to pay online, and there are no fake agent rankings.
