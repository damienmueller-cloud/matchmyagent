About MatchMyAgent

Independent agent matching with honest seller education. Starting in Newcastle & the Hunter — built to expand across Australia.

MatchMyAgent (MatchMyAgent) helps sellers understand how to choose a local agent, then request an independent match. Pilot areas: Newcastle, Lake Macquarie, Maitland, Cessnock and Port Stephens — with more regions coming.

Offer in plain terms: choose your council and locality, submit one request, and connect with one selected matched agent. This website publishes education so that step is informed — not sold with fake awards.

The interactive matcher remains available as a product experience. This GoDaddy site is the MatchMyAgent branded home for education + enquire.

We do not publish fabricated rankings. Educational content is not a substitute for licensed real-estate advice.
