What this is

MatchMyAgent compares and vets local agents, then matches people selling a house with the best agent in their area — free for sellers, anywhere in Australia. We also publish short guides if you want to read first.

What you can tell us

Your suburb is enough to start. You can also tell us when you might sell (1–3 months, 3–6 months, 6–12 months, or just researching) and a few optional property facts like bedrooms, pool and renovations. These help your matched agent.

Who sees your details

Your enquiry may be shared with your matched agent so they can be introduced to you. We do not sell your personal information. See Privacy.

What this is not

Not a property listing site. Not a place to pay online. Not fake agent rankings. Guides are general info — not a substitute for licensed advice.
